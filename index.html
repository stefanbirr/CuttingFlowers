How to cut

  1. Wait for the bloom. Stems sprout, open, then wither. The ring around a flower closes as it peaks — cut on the bright ring.
  2. Match the angle. The badge on each stem shows the blade angle against the stem: / slanted 45°, — straight across, \ shallow 30°.
  3. Hit the cut point. A pale band marks where the stem should be severed. Low bands mean long stems for the vase.
  4. Mind the speed. Dots on the badge: one = slow and careful, two = steady, three = a fast snap.
  5. Some stems need a shape. A curve, a saw-toothed zigzag, or two crossing strokes for woody stems.
  6. Leave the nettles alone. Three stings and the round is over.
